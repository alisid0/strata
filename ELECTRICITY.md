# Electricity & Circuits — topic spine

A new standalone physics path (not nested in Coordinate Geometry — circuit algebra
isn't graph-native the way fields are; this earns its own path, parallel to the
existing Forces/Energy/Gravity paths). Internal index prefix: **EL#** (temporary,
same convention as Coordinate Geometry's CG# — the real term is BB once locked).

## Tier 0 — Charge and current (EL1–7)

1. What electric charge is (static electricity, balloon-on-hair intuition)
2. Two kinds of charge — positive and negative
3. Like charges repel, opposite charges attract
4. Conductors vs insulators — why charge moves through some materials and not others
5. What electric current is — charge in motion
6. Current is measured in amperes
7. What voltage is — the "push" behind the current (potential difference)

## Tier 1 — Resistance, Ohm's Law, circuits (EL8–15)

8. The water-pipe analogy: voltage = pressure, current = flow, resistance = pipe-narrowness
9. What resistance is
10. Ohm's Law: V = IR
11. Worked example: finding current from voltage and resistance
12. Series circuits — current is the same all the way around
13. Parallel circuits — voltage is the same across every branch
14. Resistors in series — resistances simply add
15. Resistors in parallel — combined resistance is less than the smallest one
