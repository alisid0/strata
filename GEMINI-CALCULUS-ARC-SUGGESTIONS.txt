--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. HARD CONSTRAINTS (please tailor every suggestion to these)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Qubix (codename Strata) is a mobile-first STEM micro-learning app. Its workshops
are small, self-contained interactive "engines." Our real, non-negotiable model:

  DO assume:
  - Svelte 4, plain JavaScript (NOT TypeScript). Pure client SPA (Vite).
  - Rendering in SVG (preferred; theme-reactive) or canvas — deterministic only.
  - Styling via our design tokens `--qx-*` (e.g. --qx-accent is a WARM RUST, not
    blue; plus --qx-green, --qx-yellow, --qx-surface, --qx-text, --qx-border…).
    Components must look right in BOTH light and dark themes.
  - Touch-first: pointer events, ~16px min touch targets.
  - Engine contract: each engine receives `prompt` (string) + `onDone(score,max)`
    and calls `onDone(1,1)` when the learner completes it. That's the whole API.
  - Reward economy: the currency is "Ws". It is POSITIVE-ONLY — we GRANT Ws, we
    never deduct them. A wrong answer gets a gentle nudge + retry, never a penalty.

  DO NOT propose (these do not exist / conflict with the product):
  - TypeScript, Tailwind classes, `bg-slate-950`/neon, `dispatch('success')`,
    hardcoded `rewardWs`.
  - A multi-level "suite wrapper", cross-level unlock gates (e.g. "≥80% to
    unlock"), or forced session length. Each workshop is an independent module
    in a catalogue; the learning sequence is provided by our topic ordering, not
    a bespoke gated container. Micro-learning = short, replayable, non-punitive.

Each engine is a Practice-style lab with 3-5 internal STAGES that retry until
correct, then a single 1/1 completion. It is wired as one module in the
Mathematics track and appears in the workshop grid with the others.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. LOCAL LINEARITY (built; awaiting your suggestions before we ship)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept: up close, a smooth curve looks like a straight line; a corner never
does (differentiable vs not).

What we built (SVG, our tokens, positive):
  - The viewport is a fixed box, but the WORLD window it covers shrinks with
    zoom: it shows ±(3/zoom) around a fixed inspection point (a, f(a)), which
    stays centred. So smooth curves visibly straighten as you zoom; |x|-style
    corners stay self-similar (a V at every zoom).
  - Zoom buttons 1× / 10× / 100× / 1000×. A crosshair marks the point.
  - At ≥100× we read out the one-sided micro-slopes (left vs right). They AGREE
    for smooth points (one slope exists) and DISAGREE for corners (no single
    slope) — reinforcing the definition of differentiability.
  - The learner then classifies: "Straight line" vs "Sharp corner". Judging is
    disabled until they've zoomed to ≥100× (so they must actually look).
  - Three stages: y=x² at x=1.5 (smooth), y=|x| at x=0 (corner), y=sin x at x=1
    (smooth). Wrong classification = a nudge ("keep zooming…"), no penalty.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. THE CAPSTONE (pending) — SECANT -> TANGENT / DERIVATIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Planned concept: anchor point A at x; drag point B (x+h) toward A; as h→0 the
secant collapses into the tangent line, and its slope is f'(x). Then sliding the
tangent across f(x) "paints" the derivative graph f'(x) below.
We have NOT built this yet and want your best thinking on it (within Section A).
