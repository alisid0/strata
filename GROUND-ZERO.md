# Ground 0 — the foundation layer

The deck is built as a pyramid. **Ground 0** is the complete foundation of pre-university STEM: every stone here gets placed before climbing to ground 1. Topics are *peers* on this layer, not a linear sequence — order of building is flexible, completeness is the goal.

Source: the two syllabus whiteboards (Maths/Physics + Chemistry). Status legend:
- ✅ **placed** — built into the deck
- 🟡 **partial** — some of it built, more owed
- ⬜ **not yet** — untouched
