# Discord Community Launch Plan

Qubix needs a small, focused community before it needs a large audience. The first Discord should be built for feedback, trust, bug reports, early testers, and visible momentum.

## Purpose

The Discord is not just a chat room. It is the launch feedback loop.

Main goals:
- Talk directly with early users.
- Watch where learners get confused.
- Collect bugs, screenshots, and content issues.
- Recruit testers for Google Play and web releases.
- Let committed early users feel like founding members.
- Turn feedback into visible product improvements.

## Server Positioning

Name:
- Qubix Community

Short description:
- Learn science, maths, chemistry, and computing in short interactive boards. Help shape the app before public launch.

Tone:
- curious
- kind
- focused
- honest
- no shame around not knowing
- no noisy hype

## Recommended Server Structure

### Start Here

`#welcome`
- What Qubix is.
- Link to the live app.
- Link to rules.
- Short intro prompt.

`#rules`
- Be respectful.
- No harassment, spam, or hate.
- No sharing private personal data.
- Keep criticism useful.
- No answer dumping for live school exams.
- Moderators can remove content or users to keep the space safe.

`#announcements`
- New BBs.
- New workshops.
- New visuals/audio.
- Play Store testing invites.
- Maintenance notes.

`#introductions`
Prompt:
> Say hi, what you are learning, and which subject you want Qubix to improve first.

### Product Feedback

`#bug-reports`
Template:
```text
Device:
Browser/app:
Where it happened:
What I expected:
What happened:
Screenshot/video:
```

`#content-feedback`
For confusing BBs, unclear floors, wrong explanations, typo reports, image/audio mismatches.

`#workshop-feedback`
For workshop difficulty, broken interactions, boring tasks, unclear scoring, missing options.

`#feature-requests`
For new topics, app ideas, reward ideas, and habit features.

### Learning Rooms

`#physics`
`#mathematics`
`#chemistry`
`#computer-science`

Use these for subject discussion and content requests.

### Early Testing

`#web-testers`
- Live link drops.
- Weekly testing tasks.
- Feedback prompts.

`#android-testers`
- Google Play internal/closed testing instructions.
- Device reports.
- Install issues.

`#release-notes`
Use a simple format:
```text
Released:
- Added:
- Fixed:
- Known issues:
- What to test:
```

### Community

`#wins`
- Users post W Score, finished workshops, streaks, and topic completions.

`#study-room`
- Quiet accountability and daily learning check-ins.

## First 30 Members Plan

Invite personally. Do not post publicly first.

Target:
- students
- tutors
- teachers
- self-taught coding learners
- parents of STEM learners
- friends who will be honest

Ask:
> I am opening a small tester Discord for Qubix. Can you try the app for 5 minutes and tell me where you got bored, confused, or impressed?

Do not ask:
> Do you like it?

Better feedback questions:
- Where did you stop?
- What was confusing?
- Which screen felt most useful?
- Did the workshop make you more confident?
- Would you come back tomorrow?
- What should exist before you recommend it?

## First Week Community Schedule

Day 1:
- Open Discord.
- Invite 10 people directly.
- Ask each to test one subject and one workshop.

Day 2:
- Fix the top 3 obvious issues.
- Post release notes.

Day 3:
- Invite 10 more testers.
- Ask them to compare BB reading vs workshops.

Day 4:
- Post a poll: which subject should get visuals/audio first?

Day 5:
- Share a small product progress update.
- Ask for Android testers.

Day 6:
- Run a 20-minute group feedback session.

Day 7:
- Publish a weekly community recap:
  - testers joined
  - bugs fixed
  - features improved
  - what is next

## App Integration

Add Discord link in:
- Settings
- Home footer or support area
- Post-workshop completion screen later
- Play Store listing support/contact section

Recommended button text:
- Join Discord
- Help shape Qubix
- Report bugs and request topics

Do not force Discord during onboarding. Mention it after the first successful workshop or in Settings.

## Automation Ideas

Use Discord bots carefully:
- Welcome message
- Rules acknowledgement
- Feedback forms
- GitHub/Vercel release notes later

Avoid:
- fake activity
- fake testimonials
- spammy DM automation
- pretending simulated app league users are real Discord members

## Moderation

Minimum rules:
- No harassment.
- No spam/self-promotion.
- No personal data sharing.
- No exam cheating requests.
- No abusive feedback.

Moderation standard:
- Criticism is welcome.
- Cruelty is not.
- Confusion reports are valuable.
- Beginner questions are never mocked.

## Launch Message Template

```text
I am opening the first small tester group for Qubix.

Qubix is a microlearning app for science, maths, chemistry, and computer science. It uses short boards plus interactive workshops to help ideas actually click.

I am not looking for praise. I am looking for honest testers.

Try it for 5 minutes and tell me:
- where you got confused
- where you got bored
- what felt useful
- what would make you come back tomorrow

Live app:
[link]

Discord:
[invite]
```

## What To Track

Community metrics:
- members joined
- active members per week
- bug reports submitted
- content feedback items
- workshop feedback items
- Play testers recruited
- issues fixed from Discord feedback

Product metrics linked to Discord:
- first workshop completion
- return within 48 hours
- feedback after first session
- subject most requested
- visuals/audio most requested

## Before Public Posting

Checklist:
- Server rules written.
- Channels created.
- Welcome message posted.
- At least one moderator assigned.
- Live app link works.
- Feedback/reporting path works.
- Known major bugs are listed honestly.
- Founder intro post is ready.
