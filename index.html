QUBIX

Learn STEM forwards. Solve it backwards.

Qubix gives difficult concepts two complementary journeys. Learn First builds the idea step by step with teaching along the way. Solve First starts with the problem, lets you uncover the pattern, and reveals the formal concept only after you have proved it.

  • Physics — units & dimensions, forces & Newton's laws, motion, measurement
  • Mathematics — coordinate geometry: the plane, lines, slope & distance
  • Chemistry — atomic structure, bonding, the mole & molecular shapes

Same concept. Opposite route. One goal: understanding that survives outside the lesson.
