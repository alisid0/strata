The system
Six states, three marks, three values — straight from the project brief.
THE SIX MASTERY STATES — earned per path, and they stack
·
Wandered
opened the path once
●
Checked
read all its boards
✓
Well read
passed the path's quiz once
↻
Recalled
came back ~7 days later
★
Mastered ×1
quiz scored ≥ 9 / 10
★★
Mastered ×2
recalled after ~28 days & ≥ 9/10
THREE MARKS — chalk-drawn, one per subject
Physics
the atom & its orbits
Mathematics
axes & a parabola
Chemistry
the flask
HOW IT ADDS UP — three values feed the score
Reading valueopening & reading boards
Testing valuepassing the quizzes
Recall valuereturning & re-passing
The leaderboard sums all three. Reading climbs fast and early; recall is the slow, valuable one that only time can earn.
