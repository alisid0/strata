Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Qubix is a microlearning app for physics, maths and chemistry. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it. We aim to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we store the email address you sign up with, and a display name if you provide one. If you sign in with Google, we receive your basic Google account details (name, email) to create your account. Your email address is held by our authentication provider and is not stored in our public app tables.
  • Learning progress. Which boards you have opened, your quiz and workshop scores, streaks, and the in-app rewards you earn. This is stored on your device (in your browser) and, if you are signed in, associated with your account so it can follow you across devices.
  • Usage analytics. We use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics to understand aggregate usage (for example, page views and general location by country). This does not identify you individually.

How we use it

  • To run the app, sign you in, and save your learning progress.
  • To understand which content and features are useful, so we can improve Qubix.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show third-party advertising.

Service providers

We rely on a few trusted providers to operate Qubix: Supabase (authentication and database), Vercel (hosting and analytics), and Google (only if you choose Google sign-in). These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms.

Your choices

  • You can use Qubix as a guest without an account; progress then stays only on your device.
  • Clearing your browser storage removes local progress on that device.
  • Download your data. When signed in, open Settings → Your data → Download my data to export a copy of everything associated with your account as a JSON file.
  • Delete your account. When signed in, open Settings → Your data → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and all associated learning data. You can also contact us using the details below to make a request.

Data retention

We keep your account and learning data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your sign-in identity and all associated learning data are removed permanently and cannot be recovered. Guest progress lives only in your browser and is never sent to us.

Changes

We may update this policy as Qubix grows. Material changes will be reflected here with a new date.
