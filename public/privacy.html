Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

Qubix is a microlearning app for physics, maths, chemistry and computing. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it. We aim to collect as little as possible.

Qubix is operated by Arcave Technologies, which is the data controller for the personal information described here.

Minimum age

You must be at least 13 years old to create a Qubix account. Qubix is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

What we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we store the email address you sign up with. If you sign in with Google, we receive your basic Google account details to create your account. Your email address is held by our authentication provider and is not copied into our application database.
  • An internal username. Each account gets an automatically generated handle derived from your account identifier, or from a display name if you chose to provide one. It is not derived from your email address.
  • Onboarding answers. Optional: an age range, a learning goal, a daily time goal, the subjects you pick, how you heard about Qubix, and your preferred learning style. You can skip these or answer "prefer not".
  • Learning progress. Which boards you have opened and how deep, quiz and workshop scores, streaks, and rewards. Stored on your device and, if you are signed in, associated with your account so it follows you across devices.
  • Activity data. Coarse records of when you used the app and roughly how long a session lasted, used to power streaks and daily goals.
  • Issue reports. If you report a problem, we store your message, the screen you were on, your browser's user-agent string, your screen size, the app version, and any screenshot you attach. Reports require an account so we can follow up.
  • Usage analytics. Privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics covering aggregate usage such as page views and general location by country. This does not identify you individually.

How we use it

  • To run the app, sign you in, and save your learning progress.
  • To respond to problems you report.
  • To understand which content and features are useful, so we can improve Qubix.

Legal basis

Under UK GDPR we process your data on the basis of contract (running the account and saving progress you asked us to save), legitimate interests (keeping the service secure, fixing problems, and understanding aggregate usage), and consent where you optionally provide onboarding answers.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show third-party advertising.

Service providers

We rely on a few trusted providers to operate Qubix: Supabase (authentication, database and file storage), Vercel (hosting and analytics), and Google (only if you choose Google sign-in). These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms and may store data outside the UK.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Your data. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone — there is no recovery period.

When you delete your account we remove your login, your profile, all learning progress, quiz and workshop attempts, streaks, rewards, activity history, and any screenshots you attached to a problem report.

Two things are not deleted, and we want to be straightforward about why:

  • Problem reports. If you reported a bug, the report itself is kept so we can still fix it — but we strip out everything that links it to you, including your account reference, your device information and any screenshot. What remains is a description of a fault, which no longer identifies you.
  • Aggregate analytics. Our usage statistics never identified you individually, so there is nothing personal in them to remove.

Copies of data may persist in our providers' routine backups for a short period before those backups are overwritten on their normal cycle.

Your choices and rights

  • You can use Qubix as a guest without an account; progress then stays only on your device.
  • Clearing your browser storage removes local progress on that device.
  • You can request a copy of everything associated with your account.
  • You can delete your account and its data. This removes your learning data, issue reports, screenshots, profile and login.

Under UK GDPR you also have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our processing of your personal data, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, contact us below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

AI-assisted content

Some Qubix learning content, images and audio are created or assisted by artificial intelligence and then reviewed. We do not use your personal information or your learning data to train AI models.

Changes

We may update this policy as Qubix grows. Material changes will be reflected here with a new date.

Contact

Questions, data requests, or account deletion: admin@arcavetech.co.uk.
